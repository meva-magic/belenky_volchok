Free Tree Sprites in PNG format, for 2D and 3D use. 
Especially useful for particle forests in an realistic art style.

You can use theese sprites for free for own projects, including commercial ones and edit and distribute them.
They are from https://klamtii.itch.io/tree-sprites
It would be nice, if you give credit or donate on itch, but you don't have to.

No warranty or liability.

The 30 Tree sprites come in groups of 3 resembling variants of the same tree. 
Two seperate sprites of one group can be used to make cross plane (non-facing) billboards in a 3D world.
In 2D worlds, use the sprites directly.

In realistic scenes, they can be used for particle-forests in the background. Some images on the itch page
show Blender scenes doing that. Note theese are CGI renders, and not realtime. 

The Groups are:
Conifers:    A, B, C
Hardwood:    D, E, F
Birch:       G, H,
Dead/Winter: I, J

For realistic scenes conifers and hardwood should each form clusters, while hardwood has dead wood mixed in.
The typical artficial conifer forest is dense and has a brown floor. Natural conifer forest (Taiga)
is more sparse and has moss covered floor. Birches grow in extreme places like swamps, bogs or near roads and rivers
or on poluted or overfertilized soil. Coniferes like it dry and don't grow near rivers, but in the mountains.
 
The Sprites themselves were made with Blender.
